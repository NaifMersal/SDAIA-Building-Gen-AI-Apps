10-day program · 5-6 hours/day · 26 sessions · ~52 h of content.

Prerequisites

What we assume you already know before day one.

  • Working Python: functions, classes, virtual environments (uv / venv).
  • Comfort with HTTP, REST APIs, and JSON payloads.
  • Awareness of what LLMs are at a user level. Deeper concepts (context engineering, RAG internals) are covered in-course.
  • Git and command-line basics.
